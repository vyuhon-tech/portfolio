How I work

Four beliefs, each learned from a mistake.

01

In enterprise software, the rules are the product. The screens come second.

I map the policy, the exceptions and the data model before I draw a frame. A beautiful screen built on the wrong rules is worse than no screen.

02

Trust is not usability.

On MDM Genie, every participant completed the flow and most still refused to enrol. Making it smoother would have changed nothing. They needed proof.

03

People will ignore an AI output they can't question.

Show the confidence, show the gaps, let people edit the inputs, and make every correction count for something. Most of the design work in an AI feature sits around the model, not in it.

04

I build what I design.

I ship production front-end code. Building a design shows me which decisions were expensive, and it's why every demo on this site is real, working code.
