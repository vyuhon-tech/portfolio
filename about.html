About

Often the only designer, so I learned to own the whole decision.

I'm Dinesh, a product designer in Bengaluru. I studied computer science and still write code, which is why the demos on this site are working software and not screenshots.

On most of my products I was the only designer. That taught me to own more than the interface. I ran discovery, wrote requirements, sequenced releases, argued about scope with engineering, and shipped front-end code. It also taught me a habit I value most: mapping the rules of a system before drawing any of its screens.

The work I'm drawn to has real stakes: device security, money, compliance, and AI that acts on people's behalf. In these products the interface decides whether people trust the system enough to let it do its job.

Trajectory

The scope I was given grew faster than my years.

  • Dec 2024 — Now

    Product Designer & acting PM · Brilyant IT Solutions

    Sole designer across a five-product enterprise suite: HR, device management, IT service, QA automation and CRM. I write requirements, design end to end and ship production UI. I built the design system the other product teams now use.

  • Aug 2023 — Nov 2024

    Product Designer · Sendcrux Labs

    Owned design across web, dashboard and mobile for two live email-infrastructure products, and took WarmupIP from concept to production.

  • Apr 2023 — Jul 2023

    Contract Product Designer · Independent

    Took procurement, marketplace and hospitality projects from brief to production handoff on my own. This is where I learned to scope ruthlessly.

  • Dec 2022 — Mar 2023

    UI/UX Designer · VictaMan Services, Aureum Network

    Where I started: mobile apps, admin dashboards and marketplace UX.

  • 2022

    B.Tech, Computer Science & Engineering

    Sri Venkatesa Perumal College.

Also shipped, without case studies: Sales CRM, IT asset management for 1,000+ assets, a customer purchase portal, employee benefits, an intranet, and client work in luxury e-commerce, healthcare operations, mentoring and hospitality.

What's next

I've had breadth. Now I want depth.

Being the only designer taught me speed, ownership and how to argue for a trade-off. I'm now looking for a team with a high bar, where I'll be challenged by designers better than me and can go deep on one hard product instead of wide across five.
